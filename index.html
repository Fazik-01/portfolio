About Me

DEVELOPER

I am currently a Frontend developer. My journey into programming began with a desire to create my own game after getting tired of bugs, limitations, and repetitive mechanics in the games I played. Although my learning process was paused for a while due to certain circumstances, I eventually returned — and this portfolio website is one of my first steps forward.
